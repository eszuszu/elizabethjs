Hello,
I'm Elizabeth, a design engineer and software developer.
I really dig 3D, computer graphics, and building for the web.

I'm on a journey to study and contribute meaningful research to the interdisciplinary fields of computer science, graphics, and human computer interaction.

In doing so, I aim to to help experiment and discover novel applications of technology, new and expressive artistic and scientific mediums and modalities, and to help create ergonomic software tools and user interfaces for empowering creators. I'm especially interested in open standards and protocols, developer experience, and participating in scaffolding the expressive, extendable clay that developers and artists can use to shape what's next on the web and in computer graphics.

To see what I'm all about, take a look at my project case studies.
